But to learn what is most necessary and most useful is not impossible even for those engaged in the work of farming, especially when they are not going to be working continuously but will have periods of rest. Now few, I know precisely, will be willing to learn in this way; but it is better that the majority of those young men who claim to philosophize should not even approach the philosopher at all — those who are unsound and soft, through whose approach philosophy is filled with stains. For among the true lovers of philosophy there is not one who would not be willing to spend his time in the countryside with a good man, even if the place should happen to be quite harsh, since he stands to gain great benefit from this way of life: from being with his teacher night and day, from being away from the evils of the city, which are a hindrance to philosophizing, and from not being able to escape notice whether he does anything well or badly — which is indeed the greatest benefit to those being educated; and to eat and drink and sleep under the watchful eye of a good man is a great benefit too. Now the things that would necessarily arise from such association in the countryside are praised by Theognis as well, in the verses where he says: "And drink and eat among these men, and sit among them, and please those whose power is great." That he means none other than good men when he says that they have great power for the benefit of human beings, if one eats and drinks and sits with them, he has made clear in these words: "For from the noble you will learn noble things; but if you mingle with the base, you will destroy even the mind you have." So let no one say that farming is a hindrance to learning or to teaching what one ought. For it does not seem to be so, if indeed it is most of all in this way, and to the greatest degree, that the learner would be with his teacher, and the teacher would keep the learner under his hand. This being so, the means of livelihood that comes from farming appears to be the most fitting for the philosopher.
